Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies and similar technologies.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites. They help websites remember your preferences and improve your browsing experience.

Cookie Categories

Necessary Cookies (Required)

Essential cookies required for the website to function properly. These cannot be disabled.

Cookies used (examples):

  • sessionid — Django session cookie used for authenticated sessions.
  • csrftoken — CSRF protection token used to secure POST requests.
  • cookie_consent — Stores the user's cookie preference choices.
  • __cf_bm, cf_clearance — Cloudflare bot/traffic management cookies.

Analytics Cookies

Cookies that help us understand how visitors interact with our website.

Cookies used: _ga, _gid, _gat

Managing Cookies

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time by opening the cookie preference center (use the "Cookie Settings" button) or through your browser settings. To withdraw or change consent for analytics or marketing cookies, open the cookie preferences and update your choices. Changes will be stored for one year.

Third-Party Cookies

We use third-party services (such as Google Analytics, Google Ads, Cloudflare, Supabase, Vercel) that may set cookies when you visit our site. These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies — please consult them for details.
